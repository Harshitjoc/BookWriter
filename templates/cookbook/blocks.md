# Blocks

The backlog. One entry per page you intend to write, in any order. The `/block` skill
reads this to know what a page is about before it drafts anything. Nothing here is
printed.

Keep the four lines:

- **What** becomes the reason it works, in one line.
- **Use when** decides whether the page is even worth writing.
- **Action** becomes the box at the bottom.
- **Band** is usually a photo: a cookbook teaches a physical thing, and a photograph
  beats a drawing of it.

## Part 1 · Heat and patience

#### Pan-seared steak
Meat · photo
- **What:** salt it early, get the pan hot, sear without moving it, then rest it so
  the juice settles back through the meat instead of running onto the board.
- **Use when:** you want a steak done right with one pan. **Skip when:** you're cooking
  a thin cut, which needs less time and none of the resting.
- **Action:** "Salt it the moment you get home, then leave it alone. The hard part is
  the five minutes after it comes off the heat."
- **Band:** photo (steak resting on a board, knife beside it)

#### The bloom
Coffee · photo
- **What:** fresh grounds hold gas that pushes water away, so a small first pour and a
  thirty second wait lets the water reach the coffee after that.
- **Use when:** your pour-over tastes thin or sour. **Skip when:** the coffee is stale,
  because a stale bed won't bloom no matter what you do.
- **Action:** "Weigh the coffee, pour twice that in water, and wait."
- **Band:** photo (water over grounds in a dripper, the bed domed into bubbles)
