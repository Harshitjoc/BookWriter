# Blocks

The backlog. One entry per thing you intend to cover, in any order. The `/block`
skill reads this to know what a page is about before it drafts anything. Nothing here
is printed.

Keep the four lines:

- **What** becomes the recognition clue, in one line.
- **Use when** decides whether the entry is worth having.
- **Action** becomes the box at the bottom.
- **Band** is a photo when the subject is physical, or the cast when the point is the
  guide itself. The cast lives in `src/engine/tools/cast.mjs`.

---

## Part 1 · In the bed

#### Tomato and basil
In the bed · photo
- **What:** basil close around a tomato and marigolds at the row's end pull pests away
  from the crop you actually want.
- **Use when:** something keeps eating your tomatoes. **Skip when:** you're growing in
  a greenhouse where the pests aren't the same.
- **Action:** "Basil at the base of each tomato, marigolds at both ends of the bed."
- **Band:** photo (tomato with basil at its base, marigolds nearby)

#### Pip the guide
Reading this book · cast
- **What:** the cast character marks the entries worth slowing down for, and the line
  beside him is the one detail that settles the identification.
- **Use when:** always, on the entries that are easy to confuse. **Skip when:** never,
  he's just a nudge.
- **Action:** "Start at the picture, not the text."
- **Band:** cast (Pip, drawn inline from cast.mjs)
