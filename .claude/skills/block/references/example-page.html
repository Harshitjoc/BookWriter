SSRF

Server-side request forgery

Say you asked the AI to add link previews. Someone pastes a URL and your app grabs that page for a thumbnail. Usually that's a normal link. But an attacker pastes a sneaky one that points back at your app's own machine.

That machine can reach private things no outsider can, like the hidden file with your keys and passwords. The attacker can't open it, but your app can. So they trick it into handing it over.

Your own app, turned against you.

TELL THE AI

I fetch user-supplied URLs. Add an SSRF guard that blocks private IPs and unsafe schemes, re-checked when the address resolves.
