# Blocks

The backlog. One entry per essay you intend to write, in any order. The `/block` skill
reads this to know what a page is about before it drafts anything. Nothing here is
printed.

Keep the four lines:

- **What** becomes the turn the essay lands on.
- **Use when** decides whether the essay earns its place.
- **Action** becomes the small experiment at the bottom.
- **Band** is a photo when the essay turns on something physical you can look at.

---

## Part 1 · Leaving

#### Packing light
Leaving · photo
- **What:** when we pack, we pack for the fear, not the weather, bringing three
  imaginary lives in one bag; the essay is about noticing which bag you're carrying.
- **Use when:** the essay has one concrete scene and one honest turn. **Skip when:**
  it has become advice, because advice is a different book.
- **Action:** "Pack the night before, then take one thing out and don't put it back."
- **Band:** photo (an open case filled with neat packing cubes)
