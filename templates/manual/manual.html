Add a webhook

Receive events the moment they happen
  1. Pick a URL on your server for the provider to call, something like /hooks/provider.
  2. Read the signing secret from the provider's dashboard and keep it in an environment variable, not in the code.
  3. On every request, compute the signature over the raw body and compare it to the header. Reject the request if they differ.
  4. Only after the signature checks out, parse the body and do the work the event asks for.
  5. Reply 200 once the work is done. Check the provider's test delivery shows succeeded.
DO THIS FIRST

Verify the signature before you parse the body. An unverified webhook is an open door to anyone who finds the URL.
