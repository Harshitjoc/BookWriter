A single idea

Say one thing, say it well.

An essay does not need to cover everything. It needs to say one thing clearly and then follow it to its end.

A paragraph earns its place by moving the idea forward. Cut what only repeats.

Say less, but say it on purpose.

Do this
Write one sentence that states your idea, and cut every sentence that does not prove it.
