Life Is Not Fair · No. 01

The Myth of Fairness

a myth we tell ourselves

Let's say you're trying to get ahead, but things don't go your way. You work hard, but you don't get the reward you expect. That's when you realize, The Myth of Fairness.

It's not that the world is out to get you, it's just that things don't always work out as planned. Sometimes, bad things happen to good people. And that's a hard truth to accept.

The Myth of Fairness, and that's okay.

Life Is Not Fair · No. 02
General

Unfair Events in Life

some people are born with more advantages than others

Let's say you're born into a family that has a lot of money. You'll likely have more opportunities than someone who's born into a poor family. That's not fair, because it's not about what you can do, it's about who your parents are.

Some people are born with more advantages than others. It's not about what they've done, but the circumstances they were born into.

Life's not fair, and it starts from the beginning.

The Role of Luck and Circumstances

things don't always go as planned, and that's just life

Even with skill, timing or luck can decide outcomes. It's not because you're not good enough, it's just that things didn't go your way.

Sometimes timing is just off. Sometimes that's just life: you can't control everything.

How you deal with it matters more than fairness.

Living a Life of Freedom

living with purpose, despite the odds
