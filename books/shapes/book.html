Resting meat

Why the same bird is tough on Monday and tender on Thursday
  1. Salt it, and do not rinse it off.
  2. Leave it uncovered in the fridge. A skin needs air to dry.
  3. Wait until the surface feels like dry paper.
  4. Cook it skin-side down, then rest it. Resting is the step that moves the juice back in.

Dry surface, uncovered air, and a rest you nearly skipped.

The bloom

The yeasts doing the work are not the ones you buy
  1. Flour and water only, mixed until no dry patches remain.
  2. Leave it somewhere cool. Sharp smell first, then fruity.
  3. Feed it again. The sour smell is the bloom arriving, not the starter spoiling.

The smell tells you when to feed it. The clock never did.

Packing cubes

A freezer you can read without opening it

Goal: cook a week of dinners in one evening, then decide nothing after eight.

  1. Cook five meals you would eat twice.
  2. Label each bag: dish, date, stove time.
  3. Freeze sauces loose from the protein.

If you cannot build dinner from the labels alone, the labels are wrong.

Golden hour

The hour that makes a photograph, and how to find it
  1. Find the hour the sun is low and behind your subject.
  2. Look for the shadow falling away from the lens.
  3. Move until that shadow goes behind the subject.
  4. Shoot with the sun just out of frame.

Find the shadow before you find the light.

Progressive overload

Add a little, sleep, then add again

Goal: add weight to your main lift every week without hurting yourself.

  1. Add weight only when last week was clean.
  2. Add the smallest amount the bar allows.

The check: this week’s weight against last week’s.

The week you stall is data, not failure.

Shapes · No. 07
Money

The emergency fund

One number, and the discipline not to touch it
  1. Count essentials only: rent, food, transport, minimum debt payments.
  2. Turn that into a number of months you could survive on.
  3. Put it in a separate account, so spending it is deliberate.
  4. Replenish it before you invest anything else.

A separate account is not a budget. It is a wall.
