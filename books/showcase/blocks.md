# Blocks — the showcase

Ten pages, ten subjects, one engine. Five carry a photograph because the thing being
taught is physical. Five carry a diagram because the thing being taught is a mechanism.

---

## Part 1 · Things you make

#### Resting meat
Cooking · photo
- **What:** heat drives the juices to the middle, so cutting straight away spills them.
- **Use when:** any roasted or seared meat. **Skip when:** thin cuts cooked in seconds.
- **Action:** five minutes for a steak, fifteen for a roast, loosely covered.
- **Band:** photo (steak resting on a board, juices gathering)

#### The bloom
Coffee · photo
- **What:** fresh grounds hold gas that pushes water away; a small first pour lets it out.
- **Use when:** any pour-over with fresh coffee. **Skip when:** the coffee is stale.
- **Action:** twice the coffee's weight in water, wait thirty seconds, then pour.
- **Band:** photo (the domed crust rising in the dripper)

#### Companion planting
Gardening · photo
- **What:** some plants confuse or distract what eats the plant next to them.
- **Use when:** a bed you keep losing to pests. **Skip when:** containers indoors.
- **Action:** basil at the base of each tomato, marigolds at both ends of the bed.
- **Band:** photo (tomato with basil around the base)

#### Packing cubes
Travel · photo
- **What:** deciding where things live once, at home, so the case cannot mix itself up.
- **Use when:** more than two stops. **Skip when:** one hotel, two nights.
- **Action:** one cube per kind of thing, and one left empty for laundry.
- **Band:** photo (open case, four neat cubes)

## Part 2 · Things you practise

#### Golden hour
Photography · photo
- **What:** low light rakes sideways and makes shadows, which is what makes things look solid.
- **Use when:** you can choose the hour. **Skip when:** you cannot, then find shade.
- **Action:** shoot the hour after sunrise or before sunset, sun to one side.
- **Band:** photo (long shadows across a field)

#### Progressive overload
Fitness · diagram
- **What:** the body only builds what it keeps being asked for, so the ask has to grow.
- **Use when:** you have trained consistently and stalled. **Skip when:** you are injured.
- **Action:** write down what you lifted, add one rep or the smallest weight next time.
- **Band:** diagram (four rising weekly bars against a flat plateau line)

#### Spaced repetition
Language · diagram
- **What:** reviewing just before you forget, with a wider gap each time, makes it stick.
- **Use when:** anything you need to recall cold. **Skip when:** you only need it once.
- **Action:** see a new word tomorrow, in three days, in a week, in three weeks.
- **Band:** diagram (four reviews on a timeline, widening gaps, a forgetting curve below)

#### The fork
Chess · diagram
- **What:** one piece attacking two things at once, so only one of them can be saved.
- **Use when:** every game. **Skip when:** never.
- **Action:** before each move, look for a square that hits two pieces.
- **Band:** diagram (a knight on a small board, two red arrows)

## Part 3 · Things you protect

#### The emergency fund
Money · diagram
- **What:** cash set aside on purpose, so an unexpected bill does not become debt.
- **Use when:** you have any income at all. **Skip when:** never.
- **Action:** work out one month of costs, open a separate account, automate a transfer.
- **Band:** diagram (a bill hits the fund, the bill is paid, no new debt)

#### Circuit breaker
Code · diagram
- **What:** after a few failures in a row, stop calling and answer with a fallback.
- **Use when:** your app depends on a service you do not control. **Skip when:** no
  external calls at all.
- **Action:** "Put a circuit breaker around my calls to this service. Open it after a few
  failures, return a fallback right away, and retry one call now and then."
- **Band:** diagram (app to breaker, failing path in red, fallback path in teal)
