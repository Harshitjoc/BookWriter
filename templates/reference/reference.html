HTTP status codes

The ones you actually meet
4xx is the request, 5xx is the server. The codes you actually meet:
CodeMeans, and what you do
400Bad request. Fix what you sent.
401Not signed in. Sign in again.
403Signed in, not allowed. Get access.
404Nothing there. Check the path.
429Too many requests. Wait, don't retry fast.
503Down or overloaded. Wait.

The gotcha: 401 and 403 are not the same. 401 is "I don't know you". 403 is "I know you, and no".

Read the first digit. Everything else is detail.

REMEMBER

Before you debug a failing call, read its first digit. A 4xx is your bug, a 5xx is theirs, and a 429 is just time.
