The book's voice.

This is an essay. It is slow, plain, and precise.

- Say one idea per page. Do not compress two ideas to save space.
- Write for someone reading carefully, not skimming a list.
- Use plain words. If a word could be shorter without losing meaning, shorten it.
- Make a claim, show why, and say what to do with it.
- No hype, no filler, no sweeping generalisations.
- End with one concrete action.
