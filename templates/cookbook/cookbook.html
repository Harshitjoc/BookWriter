Pan-seared steak recipe: salt the steak early, get the pan properly hot, sear without moving it, then rest it so the juice settles back through the meat instead of running onto the board.

The weeknight pan · No. 01
Meat

Pan-seared steak

Rest it, and the juice stays inside
  • one steak, about an inch thick
  • salt, more than feels right
  • a heavy pan, properly hot
  1. Salt the steak and leave it on the counter for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the pan until a drop of water skitters and vanishes.
  3. Sear 3 minutes a side. Don't move it while it cooks.
  4. Rest it 5 minutes on a warm plate, then cut.

Waiting is part of the cooking.

The bloom recipe: fresh coffee grounds are full of gas that pushes water away. A small first pour and a thirty second wait lets the gas escape, so the rest of the water can actually reach the coffee.

The weeknight pan · No. 02
Coffee

The bloom

The first pour, and why it is small
  • 18g coffee, ground medium
  • 300g water, just off the boil
  • a dripper and a paper filter
  1. Rinse the paper filter, then add the grounds.
  2. Pour 36g of water, twice the coffee's weight, over all of it.
  3. Wait 30 seconds. The bed puffs up and the bubbles stop.
  4. Pour the rest in slow circles, then let it drain.

Thirty seconds now, and the rest of the pour finally works.
